PRIVACY AND PROTECTION

Managed via Navigation Bar

Protection level and wavelength (Light, Balanced, or Extra) are configured directly from the top Navigation Bar pill. Advanced granular per-site rules, exceptions, and tracker telemetry dashboards: Coming in v0.3.

Close tabs and clear cookies, storage, and cache?

Normal-window site choices are saved. Private-window choices end when all private windows close. Notifications require Blackout to be running; delivery while the app is closed is not supported.

Site permissions

Sites must ask before using your microphone or camera. Permission lasts for the current page and is cleared on navigation. Windows device privacy settings also apply.

An indicator appears in the toolbar when access is permitted. It stays visible until you stop access or leave the page, even if the site stops recording. These choices apply to this browser session. Location and screen sharing remain blocked. Website notifications require your approval. Website requests receive Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals; these do not hide your IP address or ISP.
